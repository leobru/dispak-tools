# KOMPON on 2248 versus the four 4001 linkers

**The 4001 copies have later explicit version numbers.** The 2248
compatibility-mode linker is closest to LINK1; the native-mode linker is
closest to LINK/BLINK. They are different builds, not relocated copies.

Zones and addresses are octal; word and procedure counts are decimal.

| Volume / zones | Program | Banner version | Pascal words | Constant area words | Framed procedures | Loaded zones |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2248 / 2540–2557 | КОМПОН.РЕЖИМ2 | 1.02/2 | 6202 | 889 | 86 | 10 |
| 2248 / 2560–2577 | КОМПОН.РЕЖИМ3 | 2.02/3 | 8375 | 2199 | 99 | 13 |
| 4001 / 0175–0214 | LINK1 | 2.40/1 | 6543 | 920 | 87 | 10 |
| 4001 / 0255–0274 | BLINK1 | 2.40/2 | 6586 | 924 | 87 | 10 |
| 4001 / 0112–0131 | LINK | 2.40/3 | 6875 | 926 | 87 | 10 |
| 4001 / 0275–0314 | BLINK | 2.40/3 | 6875 | 926 | 87 | 10 |

Pascal words include the entry trampoline and startup, excluding the loader
and constant area. The constant area includes literals and any PMD tables;
its larger size need not mean more program literals.

## Layout

| Program | Pascal entry | Constant pool | P/1D | First runtime frame helper | FREE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| КОМПОН.РЕЖИМ2 | 02011 | 16103 | 17674 | 20633 | 24320 |
| КОМПОН.РЕЖИМ3 | 02011 | 22300 | 26527 | 27467 | 33712 |
| LINK1 | 02012 | 16631 | 20461 | 21426 | 24716 |
| BLINK1 | 02012 | 16704 | 20540 | 21505 | 24736 |
| LINK | 02012 | 17345 | 21203 | 22150 | 25524 |
| BLINK | 02012 | 17345 | 21203 | 22150 | 25465 |

2248 uses the older nine-word PASCODER loader; the four 4001 copies use
the ten-word alternate loader. P/BOOT loads 10 zones for РЕЖИМ2 and all four
4001 programs, and 13 for РЕЖИМ3. All six catalog allocations are 16 zones.
Allocated tail data beyond the loaded extent is not part of the executable.

For example, 4001 LINK also contains a `2.30/3` banner at address 26171,
outside its loaded extent ending at 26000. Its active banner, in the
constant area at 20671, identifies version 2.40/3.

## Branch correspondence

The compatibility-mode 2248 program resembles LINK1 more closely than the
other branches. Its use of the integer bound 32768 agrees with LINK1:
the subtraction checks at 04247 and 04324 correspond to LINK1's checks at
04317 and 04403. BLINK1 instead uses 134217728 at those two locations,
as documented in its recovered wider-address handling.

Thus the `/2` in the old `1.02/2` banner should not by itself be interpreted
as identifying the later BLINK1 branch. Both 2248 programs stamp generated
objects `KOMПOH-1`; the 4001 stamps are respectively `KOMПOH-1`,
`KOMПOH-2`, `KOMПOH-3`, and `KOMПOH-3`.

РЕЖИМ3 resembles LINK/BLINK more closely and uses the 134217728 bound in
its input-address checks at 06134 and 06235. Its larger code size partly
reflects debug instrumentation and extra dump routines; size alone would
give a misleading chronology.

For a structural comparison, instructions were normalized by omitting
UTC 0, replacing M10 operands with their raw constant values, and replacing
long address operands at or above 02000 with an address marker. Registers,
opcodes, and other operands were retained. SequenceMatcher gives:

| 2248 program | LINK1 | BLINK1 | LINK | BLINK |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| РЕЖИМ2 | 82.81% | 82.55% | 78.93% | 78.93% |
| РЕЖИМ3 | 69.63% | 69.68% | 74.08% | 74.08% |

These are instruction-sequence similarity scores, not percentages of
equivalent code or validated relocation records. They support branch
identification but do not establish interchangeability.

The two 4001 native-mode applications LINK and BLINK differ in 579 words,
with their already established differences confined to relocated service
addresses. Their normalized sequences in this comparison are identical.

## Original names recovered from РЕЖИМ3

**2248 РЕЖИМ3 retains 100 PMD scope tables with original identifiers and
source line numbers, containing 386 variable descriptors.** Eleven scopes
are named `DUMP…`. No corresponding PMD scope references were found in
РЕЖИМ2 or the four 4001 applications by this scanner.

Examples, decoded from six-bit TEXT:

| PMD table | Original identifier | Source line |
|---|---|---:|
| 22304 | ПEЧBPE | 731 |
| 22324 | ПEЧИMЯOБ | 746 |
| 22341 | ДЛИПOЛИM | 763 |
| 22350 | DUMPOM | 805 |
| 22766 | ПOИCKИME | 1009 |
| 22777 | BHECИИMЯ | 1024 |
| 23017 | SFILE | 1063 |
| 23034 | ASSIGN | 1081 |

[2248-mode3-pmd.json](2248-mode3-pmd.json) preserves all decoded scope
names, line numbers, variable names, and raw descriptors. These can help
identify original names in the recovered 4001 sources, after matching the
individual routines. No existing Pascal identifiers were renamed here.

## Reproduce

```sh
python3 runtime/linker-runtime/compare2248.py
```

[comparison2248.json](comparison2248.json) contains the complete header,
layout, bound-use, and pairwise similarity results. Binary extracts remain
under `runtime/build/linker-2248/` and can be recreated with `besmtool`.
All source volumes are read-only inputs. No whole-volume hashes or volume
modifications are made.
